## The Six Levels

**L0 - Reactive Tool (0-5):** Responds to prompts but takes no autonomous action.

**L1 - Guided Assistant (6-11):** Executes tasks you assigns, one step at a time, within narrow domains.

**L2 - Adaptive Collaborator (12-17):** Proposes and executes multi-step plans with your approval.

**L3 - Domain Specialist (18-23):** Handles domain-specific workflows independently with dynamic replanning.

**L4 - Autonomous Operator (24-28):** Manages complex cross-domain workflows with self-correction and enterprise safety.

**L5 - Strategic Agent (29-32):** Fully self-directed — sets own goals, operates continuously across systems.

## Eight Scored Dimensions (each 0-4, total 0-32)

1. **Action Capability** — what the system can actually do in the world
2. **Autonomy** — degree of independence from human direction
3. **Planning & Reasoning** — ability to decompose goals and sequence actions
4. **Adaptation & Recovery** — handling failures and changing conditions
5. **State & Continuity** — memory across interactions and sessions
6. **Reliability** — consistency of correct behavior
7. **Interoperability** — ability to work with other systems and tools
8. **Safety & Observability** — how well its behavior can be monitored and constrained

## The ReAct Loop

Agentic systems run a cycle: decides what to do next, takes a real action, observes the result, and decides again. This is the **ReAct loop** (Reasoning + Acting). The difference from a tool is that a tool waits for the next prompt; an agent decides the next prompt itself.
