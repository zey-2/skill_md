## How AI Erodes Human Agency

The RAND report maps several mechanisms:

### Choice Architecture
AI systems shape the options presented to users. When a recommendation system or autocomplete narrows the visible choice set, the human's effective agency is reduced even if they can technically override any individual suggestion.

### Skill Atrophy
As AI performs cognitive tasks on behalf of users, the underlying human skills degrade. Writing, coding, analysis — all atrophy when outsourced. This creates a dependency loop: the worse you get at the task, the more you need the AI, the less you practice.

### Preference Shaping
AI systems trained on human preferences also shape them. Recommendation engines don't just serve preferences; they form them. Over time, the human's "own" preferences become partially authored by the system.

### The Erosion is Incremental
No single interaction strips agency. The erosion is cumulative — each delegation is small, rational, and individually beneficial. The aggregate effect is a shift in who controls outcomes.

## Key Insight

The danger isn't AI seizing agency by force. It's humans surrendering it through a thousand rational micro-decisions, each one individually sensible.
